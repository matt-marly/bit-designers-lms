# Your first public contribution act

Three parts, all due **Sunday, 23:59 WAT**.

## 1. Join the Bitcoin Design Community Discord + introduce yourself

Via the join page's current invite (https://bitcoin.design/join/). Post an intro in their intro channel: who you are, where you design from, one line on your published audit (link it). Screenshot your intro for submission.

## 2. Comment substantively on one live design issue

Pick 2 candidate projects from the Playbook's hunting grounds (or the #contributions verified picks). On ONE live issue in one of them, post a comment that adds evidence: your audit findings, a device-context observation, an annotated screenshot. The GitHub for Designers doc, Section 4, is the anatomy. Submit the comment link.

## 3. Draft your Gate 4 scope

One paragraph in #contributions: which audit finding, which project, which rung of the ladder (docs improvement or flow proposal), and the smallest version of it. Facilitator + mentor feedback lands there before Week 9 begins.

## Submission

All three links/screenshots marks this lesson complete and unlocks Lesson 8: Your First Contribution (GATE 4).
