# Wireframe v1 of your full flow

Produce the complete skeleton of your chosen flow in the cohort Figma team, at low/mid fidelity. **Structure over polish:** boxes, real words, honest states.

## Requirements

- Every screen of the flow, beginning to end, including at minimum the key loading, error/failure, and empty states (happy-path-only wireframes get returned)
- **Real copy, not lorem ipsum.** In Bitcoin UX the words are the design. Write the actual warning, the actual fee line, the actual failure message — even roughly.
- At least one pattern from this week's session deliberately applied and labeled with a sticky note saying which and why
- A decisions log frame: 5+ bullet decisions made so far, each with its reason (this becomes your crit narration at Gate 2)

## Also this week

Attend or watch the midweek crit call. During project weeks it's the main feedback channel, and feedback given there is assumed known at Gate 2.
