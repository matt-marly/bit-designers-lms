# Why Bitcoin, in your own words

Write a one-page explainer (400–600 words) aimed at a fellow designer who has never touched Bitcoin — someone exactly like you two weeks ago.

## Requirements

Your explainer must:

1. **Name at least two specific failures of money** as experienced in your country (your own story or a documented one — the Q&A discussion is your raw material)
2. **Explain why Bitcoin was created, in plain language**, without the words "blockchain," "cryptography," or "mining"
3. **Connect at least two of the five principles** to something a designer would care about
4. **Contain zero price talk**

## Format

Google Doc, Notion page, or PDF — your choice. Write like you design: clear hierarchy, no filler.

## Submission

Due **Sunday, 23:59 WAT**. Submit on this lesson page — link + 2–3 sentence reflection on what was hardest to explain.
