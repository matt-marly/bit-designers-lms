# GATE 4: Contribution + case study, presented at Demo Day

## The contribution (submit link by **Friday, 23:59 WAT**)

One formally submitted design contribution, built from your audit findings, at rung 2 or 3 of the ladder:

- An issue comment with a full proposal, or
- A Design Guide / docs improvement PR, or
- A flow proposal issue (the standard shape)

Submitted, not necessarily merged. You are graded on evidence linkage, scope realism, convention fit, and communication quality — the things you control. Follow the Playbook template for your chosen rung.

## The case study (submit link by **Sunday, 23:59 WAT**)

Written to the template (Lesson tab, Part B), published to your portfolio and the BitDesigners feed. Links verified, tone check passed.

## Demo Day (end of Week 9)

Present both in 5 minutes. Slides in the cohort deck by **Wednesday's rehearsal call** (required attendance).

## Review

Submitted → In review → **Approved** / **Revise & resubmit**.

Approval = graduation. Approved contributions enter the mentorship track, which carries them toward merge beyond the program.

## Why it matters

This is the program's entire thesis in one artifact: an African designer, working from published evidence, contributing in public to real Bitcoin software. Everything before this week was practice. This is the thing itself.
