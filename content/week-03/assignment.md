# Choose your flow + 3-wallet competitive scan

## Part 1: Commit to a flow

Post your choice (Onboarding / Send / Backup) with two sentences on why, in #flow-redesign, for facilitator sign-off.

## Part 2: Competitive scan

In the cohort Figma team, study how three real wallets handle your chosen flow:

- Capture the complete flow from each wallet as screenshots (every screen, including errors you can trigger safely)
- Annotate directly on the frames: what works, what fails, and why — with every annotation tied to a principle (Lesson 1), a heuristic (NN/g), or a Design Guide pattern. "Feels clean" is not an annotation.
- Minimum **15 substantive annotations** across the three wallets
- Wallet picks: at least one wallet used in your own market; at least one non-custodial. (Your Lesson 2 lab wallets count.)
- End with a synthesis frame: the 3–5 opportunities your redesign will chase
