## Gate 3 deliverable (due Sunday of Week 7, 23:59 WAT)

- A rubric-scored audit of one real open-source Bitcoin product, following the report template
- Primary research included: at least two user interviews or one moderated usability test (3–5 participants), run under the Consent Kit
- Findings synthesized and severity-ranked, with your annotated Figma appendix linked
- Publishing checklist fully checked
- Published to the BitDesigners audit feed on approval

## Review

Submitted → In review → **Approved** (Phase 4 unlocks) / **Revise & resubmit** (targeted fixes, one-week re-review).

The most common revise reasons, in order: missing evidence for scores, identifiable participants, no documented strengths.

## Why it matters

This is your first public professional artifact in the Bitcoin design world — and your Lesson 8 contribution will be built from one of its findings. Audit like the maintainers will read it. They will.
